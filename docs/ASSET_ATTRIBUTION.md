- Intended use: welcome/About surfaces and shared cross-project developer identity

- Licence scope: redistribution, commercial use, and adaptation are permitted subject to the CC BY 4.0 attribution requirements; no NonCommercial or NoDerivatives restriction applies
- Current repository status: not yet bundled
- Remaining provenance gate: record the exact approved asset's public-safe creator/source provenance and stable repository asset identity before adding the binary to Git, an APK, F-Droid metadata, or store materials

The project owner has selected CC BY 4.0 for the shared K2040 avatar so attribution remains required while the asset remains freely redistributable and adaptable. F-Droid's current `Non-Free Assets` guidance identifies Creative Commons NonCommercial or NoDerivatives restrictions as non-free examples; this asset uses neither restriction.

Do not copy private storage locations, private identifiers, recovery records, or unpublished asset metadata into the repository. Before bundling the avatar, add the exact approved binary through normal review, record its public-safe provenance and checksum/identity, retain the CC BY 4.0 attribution notice, and recheck the current F-Droid inclusion/anti-feature rules.
